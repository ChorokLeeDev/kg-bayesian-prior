\subsection{Experimental Setup}

\paragraph{Datasets.}
Static benchmarks with simulated temporal OOD: \textbf{FB15k-237} (14.5K entities, 237 relations), \textbf{WN18RR} (40.9K, 11). Temporal KGs with ground-truth timestamps: \textbf{ICEWS14} (7.1K, 230), \textbf{ICEWS18} (23K, 256), where OOD categories emerge from chronological splits---breaking the definitional coupling between coverage and OOD labels.

\paragraph{Baselines.}
UKGE~\citep{chen2019embedding}, Energy~\citep{liu2020energy}, Deep Ensembles~\citep{lakshminarayanan2017simple}, and single signals ($U_{\text{sem}}$, $U_{\text{str}}$). All share identical splits, hyperparameters (dim$=$100, lr$=10^{-3}$, 30 epochs), and evaluation protocol.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
AUROC (primary). WN18RR/ICEWS18 use 5 seeds; FB15k-237/ICEWS14 use 10 seeds; YAGO3-10 uses 3 seeds (directionally consistent but less statistically precise; Appendix~\ref{app:per_seed}). Statistical significance: CAGP vs.\ baselines $p < 0.001$; effect sizes $d > 5$ (Appendix~\ref{app:significance}).

\textbf{Key findings} (Table~\ref{tab:temporal_ood}). \emph{Non-circular evidence (ICEWS14/18):} On temporal KGs with ground-truth timestamps, $U_{\text{str}}$ achieves 0.99 AUROC while baselines achieve 0.38--0.87. This is not circular---OOD is defined by timestamp, not coverage. On ICEWS, CAGP$\approx U_{\text{str}}$ because $\rho \approx 0$ (nearly all emerging entities lack coverage), leaving no room for semantic tiebreaking---exactly as Theorem~\ref{thm:info_decomp}(iii) predicts. \emph{Semantic gains on static benchmarks:} Where $\rho > 0$ (34--66\% coverage overlap), semantic variance adds +8--11pp on emerging entities over coverage alone (WN18RR: +8pp, FB15k-237: +11pp; both $p < 0.001$). \emph{Circularity disclosure:} On static benchmarks, novel-context AUROC$=$1.0 by construction; these are diagnostic, not performance claims.

Table~\ref{tab:confident_wrong} reveals score-based methods are \emph{most confident} on zero-evidence queries. Among Energy's top-100 predictions, zero-evidence queries appear at 2.4$\times$ the base rate (78\% vs 32\% baseline). This elevation---not the absolute percentage---is the key finding: methods systematically assign highest confidence to queries with no training evidence. \textbf{Coverage tracking directly addresses this}: all zero-evidence queries would be flagged as high uncertainty.

Table~\ref{tab:complementarity} confirms Empirical Regularity~1: $U_{\text{sem}}$ achieves 0.34--0.40 on novel contexts (significantly below 0.5, $p < 10^{-3}$). This \emph{anti-predictive} behavior (AUROC $<$ 0.5 means high uncertainty correlates with ID, not OOD) occurs because novel-context entities are high-frequency (by definition, $\freq(e) > \tau$), hence receive \emph{lower} variance than ID entities---the opposite of what OOD detection requires. This is consistent with Theorem~\ref{thm:impossibility}'s bound: the theorem guarantees AUROC $\leq$ 0.5, and the observed values fall within this bound.

\subsection{Negative Result: GDELT Role-Shift}
\label{sec:gdelt}

CAGP fails when coverage overlap is high. On GDELT role-shift ($\rho = 0.84$, 500K entities): semantic achieves 0.50 AUROC (random), structural achieves 0.58, CAGP achieves 0.57. When 84\% of OOD has full coverage, neither signal works. Role-shift OOD requires relation-conditioned methods beyond our scope.

\subsection{Cross-Domain: Biomedical KGs and Foundation Models}

\paragraph{Hetionet.} On drug-disease relations, 61.7\% are novel contexts. Energy achieves 0.50 (random); Coverage achieves 1.00. Under inductive splits (new diseases), 99\% of predictions involve coverage blind spots.

\paragraph{ULTRA.} The KG foundation model achieves 0.29 AUROC on novel contexts---\emph{below random}---confirming Theorem~\ref{thm:embedding_impossibility}: scale does not fix the blind spot.

\subsection{Ablations}

Removing $\mathcal{L}_{\text{margin}}$ causes $<$0.1pp change; reparameterization is essential (without it: 0.92$\to$0.72, $p < 0.001$). Standard random-corruption OOD: CAGP ties best single signal (Appendix~\ref{app:standard_ood_full}).
