% Conclusion

This paper identifies a fundamental limitation in probabilistic knowledge graph embeddings: learned variances are relation-agnostic and cannot capture structural uncertainty.
The semantic-structural decomposition provides a principled framework explaining why existing uncertainty methods fail under temporal distribution shift---they conflate two orthogonal uncertainty types that require separate treatment.

\paragraph{Key Findings.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Score-based methods fail on temporal OOD}: UKGE and Energy achieve 0.99 AUROC on random corruptions but only 0.52--0.55 on temporal shift---they detect implausibility, not unfamiliarity
    \item \textbf{Complementarity validated}: GP excels on emerging entities (0.83); coverage excels on novel contexts (1.00); neither alone suffices
    \item \textbf{Real temporal validation}: On ICEWS14 with ground-truth timestamps, our method achieves 0.91 AUROC vs 0.52--0.58 for baselines
    \item \textbf{Simple algorithms work}: CAGP (linear combination) achieves most of the gains; fancier variants add marginal improvements
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{NLP integration}: All experiments use KG benchmarks. The QA task is simulated link prediction, not natural language QA. Integration with KGQA systems (e.g., EmbedKGQA) is future work.
    \item \textbf{Adversarial robustness}: Our method targets natural temporal shift, not adversarial attacks. While we achieve 0.78 AUROC on adversarial settings (Table~\ref{tab:ablation}), this remains below temporal OOD performance ($>$0.90).
    \item \textbf{Storage}: Coverage requires $O(|\mathcal{E}| \times |\mathcal{R}|)$ storage, though sparse ($<$5\% non-zero).
    \item \textbf{Link prediction quality}: Base MRR 0.255 on FB15k-237 vs SOTA $\sim$0.35. Uncertainty is orthogonal to prediction accuracy.
    \item \textbf{Algorithmic simplicity}: The core CAGP algorithm is straightforward; our contribution is the analysis and framework, not algorithmic novelty.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Future Work.}
Integration with end-to-end KGQA systems; inductive settings with truly unseen entities; calibration for downstream decision-making.
