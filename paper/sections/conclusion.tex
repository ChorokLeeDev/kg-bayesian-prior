% Conclusion

This paper identifies a fundamental limitation in probabilistic knowledge graph embeddings: learned variances are relation-agnostic and cannot capture structural uncertainty.
The semantic-structural decomposition provides a principled framework explaining why existing uncertainty methods underperform on knowledge graphs---they conflate two orthogonal uncertainty types that require separate treatment.

CAGP, a simple linear combination of both signals, achieves 0.87--0.96 AUROC with consistent synergy across benchmarks.
The primary contribution is the decomposition framework itself, not the combination algorithm.

\paragraph{Key Findings.}
Temporal and adversarial OOD experiments reveal the complementarity structure:
\begin{itemize}
    \item GP excels at detecting \emph{emerging entities} (0.83 vs 0.78 for coverage)
    \item Coverage excels at detecting \emph{novel relational contexts} (1.00 vs 0.42 for GP)
    \item Under adversarial corruption, CAGP shows positive synergy in 4/5 settings
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Adversarial vulnerability}: On relation-plausible corruptions (designed to fool coverage), CAGP shows negative synergy ($-$11\%). Simple averaging hurts when both signals are adversarially degraded. Adaptive weighting or learned combination may help.
    \item \textbf{Storage}: The coverage matrix requires $O(|\mathcal{E}| \times |\mathcal{R}|)$ storage. For YAGO3-10 (123K entities, 37 relations), this is 4.5M entries, though typically $<$5\% are non-zero and sparse representations suffice.
    \item \textbf{Link prediction}: Our base model achieves MRR 0.255 on FB15k-237, below state-of-the-art ($\sim$0.35). The uncertainty contribution is orthogonal to link prediction quality, but stronger base models may yield different uncertainty dynamics.
    \item \textbf{GP signal strength}: GP-only achieves 0.54 AUROC on overall temporal OOD, suggesting the learned variance captures limited information beyond entity frequency. The reparameterization trick helps but does not fully exploit relational structure.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Future Work.}
Learning adaptive combination weights (e.g., attention over signals); extending to inductive settings with unseen entities; investigating calibration properties for downstream decision-making; and scaling to web-scale knowledge graphs.
