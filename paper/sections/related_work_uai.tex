\paragraph{Uncertainty in Knowledge Graph Embeddings.}
Probabilistic approaches to KG embeddings have gained attention for capturing epistemic uncertainty.
UKGE~\citep{chen2019embedding} associates confidence scores with triples, modeling triple-level reliability.
BEUrRE~\citep{chen2021probabilistic} uses box embeddings where box volume indicates uncertainty.
GP-KGE~\citep{Chen2021PERM} learns Gaussian distributions over entity embeddings.
The limitation identified in this work---that learned variances are relation-agnostic---applies to all these methods, explaining their poor performance on temporal distribution shift.

\paragraph{OOD Detection.}
MC Dropout~\citep{gal2016dropout} approximates Bayesian inference via dropout at test time.
Deep Ensembles~\citep{lakshminarayanan2017simple} aggregate predictions from independently trained models.
Energy-based methods~\citep{liu2020energy} use output magnitudes for OOD scoring.
When applied to KG embeddings, these methods inherit relation-agnostic uncertainty: they capture model uncertainty but not structural observation patterns.
Our work shows that for KGs specifically, structural uncertainty from co-occurrence patterns is essential.

\paragraph{Graph-Based Uncertainty.}
Graph Posterior Networks~\citep{stadler2021graph} propagate uncertainty through graph structure for node classification.
GGPN~\citep{chen2022graph} extends this to knowledge graphs.
These methods treat edges homogeneously, missing the heterogeneous nature of KG relations.
An entity may be well-observed for some relations but completely unseen for others---a distinction homogeneous propagation cannot capture.

\paragraph{Calibration.}
Calibration ensures predicted probabilities match empirical frequencies~\citep{guo2017calibration}.
Recent work~\citep{rao2024knowledge} studies calibration specifically for KG link prediction.
Our decomposition is orthogonal to calibration: we separate uncertainty sources, while calibration adjusts confidence magnitudes.
The approaches are complementary---CAGP could be post-hoc calibrated.

\paragraph{Coverage and Sparsity.}
Entity coverage statistics are used for negative sampling~\citep{sun2019rotate} and bias analysis~\citep{akrami2020realistic}.
Prior work notes that KG incompleteness affects evaluation~\citep{safavi2020codex}.
We repurpose coverage as a first-class uncertainty signal, showing it captures structural uncertainty that learned variances miss.

\paragraph{Temporal Knowledge Graphs.}
Temporal KG reasoning~\citep{garcia2018learning} models time-evolving facts.
Our work addresses a different problem: detecting when queries fall outside the training distribution due to temporal shift, rather than explicitly modeling temporal dynamics.

\paragraph{Positioning.}
Prior work treats uncertainty as a single quantity to learn end-to-end.
We show KG uncertainty decomposes into semantic (entity-level) and structural (relation-specific) components.
The decomposition is not merely empirically useful but theoretically grounded: Theorem~\ref{thm:complementarity} proves neither signal alone suffices and characterizes when combination helps.
